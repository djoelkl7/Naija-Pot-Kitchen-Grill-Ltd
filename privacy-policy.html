Privacy Policy

How NAIJA POT KITCHEN & GRILL LTD protects your personal information and respects your privacy.

1. Introduction

NAIJA POT KITCHEN & GRILL LTD ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, handle, and safeguard customer information in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. Information We Collect

When you communicate with us via telephone, WhatsApp, or email for table reservations, menu enquiries, food orders, or catering bookings, we may collect:

  • Your contact name and telephone number.
  • Your delivery address or venue location (for catering/delivery).
  • Food preferences, dietary allergies, and order details.
  • Correspondence history required to fulfil your order.

3. How We Use Your Data

We process your personal details strictly to:

  • Coordinate, prepare, and deliver your food orders.
  • Respond promptly to enquiries made through WhatsApp (+44 7342 229865) or Phone (+44 7444 614764).
  • Comply with UK Food Standards Agency and health hygiene traceability requirements.

4. WhatsApp & Third-Party Services

When ordering via WhatsApp, your interactions are also subject to Meta/WhatsApp's privacy policy. We do not sell, rent, or distribute customer details to third parties for commercial marketing.

5. Payment Security

We do not store complete debit or credit card details on this website. In-person transactions and approved remote payments are processed through secure, PCI-DSS compliant payment terminals and banking channels.
